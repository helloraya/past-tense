A Past Tense Investigation

"A diamond was stolen at 8.00 PM last night!
Are you the thief?" 🙀

The Interrogation Room 🔦

Question 1 (Action) 🏃‍♂️

"Did you steal the diamond?"

Question 2 (State/Location) 📍

"Were you at the museum last night?"

Alibi Analysis 📝

(Click the boxes to scan the answers!)

"No, I didn't steal it!"

✅ CORRECT! (Negative Verbal)
'Didn't' is used, so the verb returns to Base Form (V1).

"I didn't stole it!"

❌ WRONG! (Double Past)
'Didn't' is already past tense, no need for another past verb like 'stole'.

"No, I wasn't at the museum!"

✅ CORRECT! (Negative Nominal)
The question asks about location using 'were', so the answer must use 'was/were'.

"I wasn't steal it!"

❌ WRONG! (Mixed up!)
Error! 'Wasn't' is for nominal sentences. You cannot combine it with an action verb like 'steal'.
